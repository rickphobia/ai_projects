# Against All TOD

A Tower Offense Defense game: one player builds a fortress, then switches sides and tries to break it, on a board that never resets.

## The run

**Run**:
One solo game, from an empty board until the player has no Lives left.
_Avoid_: Game, session, campaign

**Round**:
One battle on the board, played as either a Defense turn or an Attack turn. The player switches sides only after winning a Round.
_Avoid_: Wave, level, match

**Defense turn**:
A Round where the player builds onto the Maze and must stop the Army from destroying the Goal.
_Avoid_: Build phase, defense round

**Attack turn**:
A Round where the player adds Squads to the Army and must destroy the Goal.
_Avoid_: Offense round, offense turn

**Life**:
One allowed failure. A Round that ends in a loss costs one Life; at zero the Run ends.
_Avoid_: Heart, retry, attempt

**Rewind**:
What follows a lost Round: the Board goes back to how it was at the start of that Round, and the player tries again with the same crate pick and gold plus some bonus gold.
_Avoid_: Retry, restart, reload

**Score**:
The number of Rounds won in a Run. A Run has no last Round; it ends only when the Lives run out.
_Avoid_: Points, high score (the stored best Score is the "best Score")

**Boss Round**:
Every fifth Round, where the game adds its own extra threat on top of the player's.
_Avoid_: Boss wave, elite round

**Curse**:
A random handicap the game puts on one side for one Round, announced before building starts. The player never chooses it.
_Avoid_: Debuff, modifier, event

**Unlock**:
A new tower, Dude kind or mutation added to what Draft Crates can offer, earned by reaching a Score milestone. Unlocks carry over between Runs.
_Avoid_: Upgrade, meta-progression, perk

## The board

**Board**:
The battlefield. Everything placed on it stays for the rest of the Run.
_Avoid_: Map, field, level

**Maze**:
Everything the defender has placed on the Board. Dudes walk the shortest open route through it, so placing pieces shapes their path. The Goal can never be sealed off.
_Avoid_: Path, lane, fortress, castle

**Goal**:
The thing the Army must destroy. It has a health bar; the Attack turn is won when it reaches zero.
_Avoid_: Base, core, castle, nexus

**Spawn line**:
The edge of the Board where the attacker places Squads.
_Avoid_: Deployment line, start zone

## The army

**Dude**:
One attacking unit.
_Avoid_: Unit, minion, soldier, creep

**Squad**:
A group of Dudes of one kind placed together on the Spawn line.
_Avoid_: Wave, troop, group

**Army**:
Every Squad placed so far. It all returns each Attack turn.
_Avoid_: Swarm, horde

**Mutation**:
A permanent change to every Dude in the Army, taken from an attack Draft Crate.
_Avoid_: Upgrade, buff, perk

**Wall-breaker**:
A Dude kind that can damage pieces of the Maze. Ordinary Dudes only walk.
_Avoid_: Siege unit, sapper (Sapper is one Wall-breaker)

## Getting things

**Draft Crate**:
The offer at the start of each Round for the side being played: three random items, of which the player takes one for free. Defense crates hold Maze pieces; attack crates hold Squads and Mutations.
_Avoid_: Loot box, pack, draft

**Defense gold**:
Gold earned by killing Dudes, spent only on the defense side.
_Avoid_: Blood money, coins

**Attack gold**:
Gold earned by damaging the Goal, spent only on the attack side.
_Avoid_: Blood money, coins

**Settings trick**:
A change made in the game's own Settings menu (Brightness, Volume, Window size, Game speed) that changes how the battle plays. Either side can buy one, each trick favours one side, and it stays until it is undone. It changes how the battle plays, never whether the player can see it.
_Avoid_: Sabotage, cheat, hack, UI weapon

**Undo**:
Reversing one Settings trick by destroying pieces of your own side, of your choice, up to the trick's price. Rare and painful on purpose.
_Avoid_: Reset, revert

**Watch speed**:
How fast the player watches a battle (1x, 2x, 4x). It never changes who wins, unlike the Game speed Settings trick.
_Avoid_: Fast-forward, game speed
