# Sync deletions on, with FolderSync signed in as the account `~/ee` uploads as

The course pairs sync deletions, and FolderSync signs in to Drive as yihung.yeapmagrick, not the owner's own account. Tested on the tablet on 23–24 Nov 2026 with a throwaway pair: with deletions off, every delete, rename or move came back as a duplicate, and a Conflict copy resolved with Keep mine came back from Drive and asked again on every sync. The owner wants no duplicates. With deletions on, Google lets only a file's owner delete it, and `~/ee` uploads as yeapmagrick, so signed in as the owner FolderSync couldn't delete `~/ee`'s files; as yeapmagrick it can, and everything the tablet uploads is then yeapmagrick's too. This replaces the deletions part of decision 0009.

**Trade-offs:** a Course folder that is present but empty on the tablet deletes from Drive everything yeapmagrick owns in it (seen in the test; a missing folder only stops the sync). The guards: files yeapmagrick doesn't own, such as the seniors' material, can't be deleted from the tablet; deleted files sit in yeapmagrick's Drive Trash for 30 days and `rclone backend untrash` restores a folder in one command (tested); ink is in the nightly ink backup; `~/ee`'s files are also on the Beelink. A seniors' file deleted on the tablet makes the pair show Failed until it is restored. The tablet's uploads use yeapmagrick's storage, not the owner's.
