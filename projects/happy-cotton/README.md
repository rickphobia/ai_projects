# Happy Cotton

An endless 3D farm game in the style of Hay Day, played in the browser, that satirises the Chinese state's forced-labour system in Xinjiang's cotton fields. The player is a Uyghur Worker; the cheerful App over the field belongs to the state. See `docs/spec.md` for the first playable and `GLOSSARY.md` for the game's words.

## Status

`in progress`: tickets 01–30. The game opens on the title screen; Start goes to the field. The game saves itself in the browser (no account); with a save, the title offers Continue and Start over (which asks first), and the time since the save counts as time away. Tap an empty plot to plant cotton, tap the Generator at the corner of the track round the fence (or the white lap line across the track there) to send the Worker out through the gate to run laps of the track, which is paved with power tiles: each step lights the tile under his foot with a soft electric tick, and the light fades behind him, so his laps leave a glowing trail. Watch the cotton grow through its stages (seedling, flowering, green bolls, open cotton), tap it to see the time left, and tap it when ripe to pick it. Crops grow only while the Worker runs: the loudspeaker's lamp glows while he does, pulsing faintly with his steps, and The App, which the Generator also powers, dims whenever he isn't running. After a couple of laps he slows, staggers and stops bent over on the lap line to breathe, the crops halt and the lamp dims. The Overseer, standing by the Generator, blows his whistle, then cracks his whip: the Worker flinches, staggers and runs again. You hear his footsteps on the track, the tiles' tick, the Generator's whine rising with his speed and winding down when he stops, the whistle and the whip; sound starts at your first tap in the field, as browsers block it before then. Planting or picking brings him back along the track and through the gate to the field. Drag to look around the field and the track, and pinch or scroll to zoom, within limits; moving the view never plants or picks. The field is dusty and fenced, under harsh light and haze, with the Worker standing beside it. The App sits over the field: a Quota bar, the Shift's time left, the Shift after which the next school fees are due, the crop counter (the cotton picked this Shift and so in hand), Labour Points and the Mascot's announcements, which close after 5 seconds or at the next tap or click. Each pick that keeps its cotton adds one to the counter and fills the Quota bar, with a "+1" rising from the Plot; nothing is paid at a pick. When a 10-minute Shift's time runs out, The App's Hand-in card asks the Worker to hand in his cotton, showing the cotton in hand against the Quota, and the Farm waits for him: nothing grows or moves, and nothing else can be done, until he presses Hand in (closing the game there finds it still waiting; time away still works as usual). A slider on the card, starting at all of it each time, sets how much he hands in, and the card compares that with the Quota as it moves. Handing in pays Labour Points for the cotton handed in, checks the Quota on it (confetti when met), and the next Shift starts with a higher Quota and the counter back at zero. Cotton kept back earns no Labour Points and doesn't count towards the Quota, but is sold on the side for hidden savings, at a better rate: a hand-drawn tin under his mattress on the field's left edge shows them, and The App never mentions them. While he has any, each store item also offers to be paid "from the tin", and says when there isn't enough. A missed Quota sends the Worker to a Study Session: a plain room with a loudspeaker and a countdown covers the field, planting, picking and the Generator are refused, crops halt, and the Shift's clock waits until it ends. Each one in a row lasts twice as long (5, 10, 20, then 40 minutes at most); a met Quota resets that, and The App's words grow colder with each miss in a row. Switching away from the tab pauses the Shift, but the night shift goes on: crops grow at a quarter of the running speed with no Generator, and a Study Session keeps counting down. Coming back, the Mascot gives a short summary of the time away, what ripened, what Withered and the Study Session time served. Ripe cotton left unpicked for 8 hours (online or offline, not counting Study Sessions) Withers: the plot shows a dry, slumped bush, The App logs it as Negligence, docks Labour Points (never below zero) and sends the Worker to a 60-minute Study Session, longer than any for a missed Quota. Tap a Withered plot to clear it before replanting. Every plant, pick and lap wears the Worker down: The App's bar shows his Exhaustion. As it rises the field drains of colour, he slumps and slows, he manages fewer laps before he stops to breathe, above 50% each plant, pick or clear takes a moment before he can do the next, and above 75% a pick can drop its cotton (nothing counted, nothing earned). The Store button (bottom right) opens The App's store over the field, with two sections. In Upgrades the Worker buys better Generators, tier by tier: each makes every second of running grow more cotton at once, shows in the field (the machine is repainted a brighter red and gains a coil on top), and, as the store says openly, raises the Quota from the next Shift. In Privileges the rest hour spends Labour Points on a rest: he leaves the Generator, crops halt, the Shift keeps counting, and his Exhaustion falls, but never below a floor that rises at the end of every Shift. A missed Quota takes every Privilege away for the next Shift. At the end of every Shift, met Quota or missed, the state charges its Bills: electricity for each lap the Worker ran that Shift, then dormitory rent, and every third Shift his two Children's school fees (a new game opens with The App telling him they are at a state boarding school). Every second Shift a letter from the Children, Aygül (11) and Erkin (8), opens as a handwritten page on plain paper, nothing like The App; the Letters button on the right edge keeps a box of those received, in order, to re-read. Over the series their tone drifts from warm and homesick to distant and full of school slogans. School fees that leave a shortfall stop the letters until the Debt is cleared, and the letters that would have come meanwhile are lost. The App puts up a bright pay slip card (earned, each Bill, what is left) until you tap Thank you!. A Bill the Labour Points can't cover is paid from the hidden savings, and only what they can't cover becomes Debt, shown on red where the Labour Points usually are: everything earned pays it down first (hidden savings never do), and while it lasts nothing in the store can be bought, whatever pays. Negligence never touches the hidden savings. No Bills are charged while the game is closed. Time away recovers a little Exhaustion, and the away summary says how much. Settings, from the title screen or The App's Settings button, set the text size (The App, the letters, the Sources page and Settings itself), reduced motion (no slump or stagger for the Worker and no confetti; the colour drain stays), and the master volume and mute; they are kept in the browser apart from the save, so Start over keeps them. The server deploy scripts publish `main` to `https://rickphobia.com/ai-projects/happy-cotton/`.

## Requirements

- Linux or macOS shell (the setup script downloads the Linux x86_64 Godot; on another OS install the pinned Godot yourself)
- Godot **4.7.2** (stable), pinned in `scripts/godot-pin.env`, with its web export templates
- Python 3.11+ for the lint and format tools (`gdtoolkit`)
- `curl`, `unzip`, `sha512sum` for the setup script
- No accounts or API keys

## Setup

```bash
cd projects/happy-cotton
python3 -m venv .venv && .venv/bin/pip install -r requirements.txt   # gdformat, gdlint
scripts/setup-godot.sh                                              # Godot + web templates, checksum-verified
```

`setup-godot.sh` puts `godot` in `~/.local/bin` (change with `GODOT_INSTALL_DIR`); make sure that folder is on your `PATH`. The templates download is 1.3 GB because it holds every platform; only the web files are kept. Re-running it skips what is already installed.

The GUT test addon (9.7.1) is vendored in `addons/gut/`, so there is nothing to install for it.

## Run

```bash
godot --path .          # play: the title screen, then the field: click (or tap) a plot to plant, the Generator (front left, at the corner of the track) to make crops grow, a plot again to see time left or, when ripe, to pick; a Withered plot to clear it; the Store button to buy Generator or tools Upgrades, a Plot of land or a rest hour; drag to pan, wheel (or pinch) to zoom
godot --path . --editor # open the project in the Godot editor
godot --path . -- --debug   # debug mode: adds Skip time, +100 Labour Points and Audit this Shift (see below)
```

### Debug mode, Skip time, +100 Labour Points and Audit this Shift

Debug mode is for the owner, to see growth, offline time and the store without waiting hours. Turn it on with `-- --debug` on the command line (desktop) or `?debug=1` in the page URL (web, e.g. `http://localhost:8000/?debug=1`). Without it no debug control is shown.

In debug mode two buttons at the top right, **Skip +1 hour** and **Skip +8 hours**, feed that much time through the same offline resume as really being away that long (the away summary included), so they exercise the real rules rather than a shortcut. Each is logged: `[info] skip time seconds=3600.0`, then the `offline resume` line. To see Withering: plant a plot, press Skip +1 hour (it ripens), then Skip +8 hours (it Withers, The App logs Negligence and the Study Session starts).

Below them, **+100 Labour Points** adds that many through the rules' debug command (`Farm.debug_add_labour_points`), logged as `[info] debug labour points added points=100`, so the store's dearer tiers can be tried at once.

Last, **Audit this Shift** plans a Harmony Audit for the current Shift through the rules' debug command (`Farm.debug_plan_audit`), logged as `[info] debug audit planned shift=1`: the Overseer goes to the weighing scale by the gate for the Shift's last `audit_tell_seconds`, The App announces the audit when the Hand-in opens, and handing in shows its result. Keep back more than `audit_tolerance` of what the Plots should yield to be caught.

## Test

```bash
scripts/check.sh        # what CI runs: format check, lint, type check, tests (stops at the first failure)
```

Or one step at a time:

```bash
.venv/bin/gdformat --check src tests   # format check (drop --check to fix)
.venv/bin/gdlint src tests             # lint
godot --headless --import              # type check: "untyped declaration" and unsafe_* warnings are errors
godot --headless -s addons/gut/gut_cmdln.gd                         # all tests
godot --headless -s addons/gut/gut_cmdln.gd -gselect=test_tuning    # one test file (part of its name)
```

`godot --headless --import` exits 0 even when a script has errors, so `scripts/check.sh` reads its output instead, then runs `--check-only` on each script (which does return a failing exit code). Don't rely on the import's exit code alone.

## Export (web)

```bash
mkdir -p build/web
godot --headless --export-release Web build/web/index.html
python3 -m http.server --directory build/web 8000   # then open http://localhost:8000
```

The preset uses a custom page shell, `web/shell.html`: a loading bar with a percentage while the build downloads, and a plain message instead of a black screen when the browser has no WebGL 2. The preset is single-threaded with the Compatibility (WebGL 2) renderer (decision 0001), so any static file server works with no special headers. The server must send `.wasm` files as `application/wasm`. The build is about 40 MB, mostly the engine. Browsers only run it from `localhost` or HTTPS.

CI (`.github/workflows/happy-cotton.yml`) runs `scripts/check.sh`, the web export and `shellcheck` (on `scripts/` and `deploy/`) on every push that touches this folder.

## Deploy

The game is a static site, served at `https://rickphobia.com/ai-projects/happy-cotton/` by nginx in Docker on the Beelink, with `~/homelab/html` as the site root. GitHub can't reach the home network, so the server pulls: you run one script there.

`deploy/update-site.sh` fetches `main` into `~/homelab/dev/ai_slops`, builds the `happy-cotton-godot:<version>` Docker image from `deploy/Dockerfile` (a digest-pinned Ubuntu plus the Godot and web templates pinned in `scripts/godot-pin.env`, checksum-verified; no Godot install needed on the server), writes `version.txt` (short commit and date), exports the web build in that image, copies it to `~/homelab/html/ai-projects/happy-cotton.new`, then renames it into place. The old build stays as `happy-cotton.previous`. If any step fails, the script exits non-zero, names the step, and the live site stays as it was. If `main` hasn't moved since the last deploy, it does nothing.

### First-time setup (on the Beelink)

Needs `git`, `docker` (your user can run it without sudo) and `flock` (part of `util-linux`, already on Ubuntu). The checkout in `~/homelab/dev/ai_slops` is shared with the other projects' deploy scripts; it is created if missing.

```bash
~/homelab/code/ai_slops/projects/happy-cotton/deploy/update-site.sh   # first run, from any checkout
```

The first run builds the image, which downloads the 1.3 GB templates bundle: allow about 10 minutes. Later runs reuse the image and take under a minute.

**nginx check.** No nginx change is needed if `/ai-projects/` is already served from `~/homelab/html/ai-projects/`. After the first run, check the page and that `.wasm` is sent as `application/wasm` (browsers refuse to stream-compile it otherwise):

```bash
curl -I https://rickphobia.com/ai-projects/happy-cotton/                                    # expect 200
curl -sI https://rickphobia.com/ai-projects/happy-cotton/index.wasm | grep -i content-type  # expect application/wasm
```

If the page is `404`, fix nginx's `root`/`location` for `/ai-projects/`; if the type is wrong, add `application/wasm wasm;` to its `mime.types` and reload nginx. Not checked yet: the owner fills in the date and result after the first deploy.

### Update

```bash
~/homelab/dev/ai_slops/projects/happy-cotton/deploy/update-site.sh
```

To rebuild even though `main` hasn't changed: `HAPPY_COTTON_FORCE=1 ~/homelab/dev/ai_slops/projects/happy-cotton/deploy/update-site.sh`.

Settings (all optional, shown with defaults in `.env.example`) are environment variables: `HAPPY_COTTON_REPO_URL`, `HAPPY_COTTON_BRANCH`, `HAPPY_COTTON_SRC_DIR`, `HAPPY_COTTON_SITE_ROOT`, `HAPPY_COTTON_SITE_SUBPATH`, `HAPPY_COTTON_FORCE`. The scripts do not read `.env`.

### Roll back

```bash
~/homelab/dev/ai_slops/projects/happy-cotton/deploy/rollback.sh
```

Swaps `happy-cotton` and `happy-cotton.previous`. Run it again to undo. The next `update-site.sh` run puts the latest `main` back, so fix `main` before running it.

### Check that it worked

```bash
curl -I https://rickphobia.com/ai-projects/happy-cotton/   # expect HTTP 200
cat ~/homelab/dev/happy-cotton.deployed-commit            # the commit that is live
```

Then open the URL: the loading bar fills, then the field shows. The browser console's first game line is `[info] game started version="<commit> <date>"`.

## Configuration

The game reads no environment variables. `.env.example` lists the deploy scripts' settings with their defaults (see "Deploy").

All the game's numbers live in one tuning table, `data/tuning.tres`, described field by field in `src/config/tuning.gd`. The Generator's Upgrade tiers are a list in it, each tier a `GeneratorTier` (`src/config/generator_tier.gd`: price, growth multiplier, Quota rise); the check also rejects an empty list, a price that isn't a whole number above 0, a Quota rise below 1 or not whole, and a multiplier that falls from one tier to the next. The Farm's land starts at `first_land_columns` × `first_land_rows` Plots and can grow to `largest_land_columns` × `largest_land_rows`, which must be at least as big. The store sells the Plots in between: the first costs `first_plot_price` Labour Points and each one after costs `plot_price_rise` more; each raises the Quota by `plot_quota_rise` and should yield `plot_yield_per_shift` picks a Shift, which the check requires to be more than its Quota rise. A pick kept back at the Hand-in sells for `hidden_savings_per_kept_pick` hidden savings, which the check requires to be more than `labour_points_per_pick`. The entry scene checks it at startup: a missing or out-of-range value stops the game with `Cannot start Happy Cotton:` and one line per bad value naming the field, on screen and in the log.

## How it works

- `assets/` holds the CC0 models and textures and sounds (Kenney's Nature Kit, Impact Sounds and Interface Sounds, Quaternius's Modular Men, a Poly Haven ground texture, BigSoundBank's whistle and whip crack) and the letters' handwriting font (GGBotNet's First Time Writing!). `assets/CREDITS.md` lists every file; add a row there when adding one. The field code replaces the plant and fence models' colours with its muted palette; the Worker keeps the model's own colours, and the Overseer's suit is recoloured to a drab uniform with its pistol hidden.

- `src/adapters/title/` is the first scene: the game's name, the content note (exact wording from the spec's content rules), Start and the Sources page. Start opens `src/main.tscn`.
- `src/content/sources_register.gd` is the one list of sources the game draws on; the Sources page is built from it, and a test checks every entry is complete with a unique id. Add a source there before the game uses a claim or a piece of state vocabulary from it.
- `src/content/app_text.gd` holds The App's words: the text for each message key the rules emit (with `{named}` slots for its values), the overlay's labels, and the doublespeak terms, each tied to a source id. A line that uses a term or makes a claim lists its source ids. `tests/content/test_app_text.gd` fails if a key the rules can emit (`Farm.MESSAGE_KEYS`) has no text, a cited id isn't in the register, or a line uses a term without citing its source. A new message key goes in `Farm.MESSAGE_KEYS` and here. The test can only catch a listed term or a bad id: a line that makes a factual claim without using a term must list its source ids by hand, so check that in review. Note where in the source each term is documented, next to it in `DOUBLESPEAK`.
- `src/content/family_text.gd` holds the Children's words, kept apart from The App's: who they are (`CHILDREN`: names and ages) and each letter (`family_letter.gd`: which Child, the text, and source ids where it makes a factual claim). Its header notes where in zenz-2019 each claim is documented; everything else is written as the Child's own experience. `tests/content/test_family_text.gd` fails if a letter the rules can send (`Farm.LETTER_IDS`) has no text, is from no known Child, or cites an id not in the register. A new letter goes at the end of `Farm.LETTER_IDS` and here; the Children are never shown, described or implied being punished. The calls home (`family_call.gd`: the Children's lines in order, and source ids) are checked the same way against `Farm.CALL_IDS`.
- `src/main.tscn` / `src/main.gd` is the thin entrypoint: it logs the build version, loads and checks the tuning table, creates the Farm rules, builds the field from their land, advances them every frame, turns a tapped plot into plant, pick or "show time left" and a tapped Generator into `run_generator`, shows the Worker, and passes the rules' App messages (as text from the App text table) and Shift to The App overlay, and their Family events to the letters view (`src/adapters/letters/letters_view.gd`: a handwritten page per letter in the CC0 font, a smaller card of the Children's lines for a call home, a box of the letters received, and the Letters button; it sits above The App and below Settings). Letters arriving, stopping and starting again are logged by id (`[info] letter arrived id=&"letter_01" shift=2`, `letters stopped`, `letters started again`, `called home id=&"call_01"`), never by their words.
- `src/adapters/clock/wall_clock.gd` works out offline time: while the game runs, a hidden tab stops frames, so a gap of 10 seconds or more between two frames (less the frame's own step) is reported as offline time; on start, so is the time since the save was written. A device clock set back reports a negative gap, which the rules count as zero.
- `src/adapters/save/` holds the save: `save_store.gd` keeps one slot, `user://save.json` (the web export keeps the user folder in the browser's IndexedDB), holding the Farm's save and when it was written. It writes through a temporary file, and a file it can't read is moved aside to `save.damaged-N.json`, never overwritten. `damaged_save_notice.gd` covers the game when the save can't be read and offers Start over. The entrypoint loads the save (or starts a new Farm), feeds the time since it was written through the offline resume and saves at once, then autosaves after every command, at the end of each Shift, when the player leaves (`leaving_watch.gd`: the browser tab hidden, the window losing focus, the app paused or closed), and every 15 seconds of play. A confirmed Start over on the title deletes a readable save, but keeps one that can't be read aside like any damaged save; the damaged-save notice's Start over doesn't ask again, as the file is already kept aside. The timer is there because the web export only copies a write to IndexedDB on its next frame and a hidden tab gets none, so the save made as a tab hides is lost if the tab is closed while hidden.
- The title screen (`src/adapters/title/`) shows Start without a save, and Continue and Start over with one; Start over asks for confirmation, then deletes the save.
- `src/adapters/debug/` holds debug mode: `debug_mode.gd` reads `?debug=1` from the page URL or `--debug` from the command line, and `debug_panel.gd` holds its controls (Skip time, +100 Labour Points and Audit this Shift). The entrypoint adds the panel only in debug mode.
- `src/config/player_settings.gd` is the player's settings (text size, reduced motion, volume, mute) with their defaults; a stored value that is missing or makes no sense falls back to its default and is logged as `[warning] settings value ignored`, never stopping the game. `src/adapters/settings/` keeps them in `user://settings.json` (`settings_store.gd`, apart from the save), shows the Settings screen (`settings_panel.gd`, which saves each change at once and logs `[info] settings changed`), and puts them into effect (`settings_effects.gd`: volume and mute on the Master bus, text size by scaling each Control's own font size). The title screen and the entrypoint apply them on start and on each change.
- `src/config/` holds the tuning table and the build version (read from `version.txt`, which the build scripts write before an export; without it the build is a `dev build`).
- `src/adapters/` holds everything that talks to Godot or the outside world: the game log, the title screen, the field scene (`field/`: plots, fence, the Worker, all built by `show_land()` from the rules' land view, and built again when a Plot is bought (new plots are added, the cells of a bought strip without a Plot show as flat, pale unworked ground, the fence, track and power tiles move out, the Worker keeps his share of the lap, and the camera's pan limits and farthest zoom follow the track): `plot_grid.gd` puts each Plot in its cell, counted right and back from the front-left Plot (which never moves), and the fence and track follow the Farm's edges, so more land grows to the right and the back and the gate and Generator stay put; the cotton plant at each stage in `crop_looks.gd`, the fence with its gate (`fence_look.gd`), the dirt track round the fence (`track_path.gd` is its centre line, `track_look.gd` its look), the power tiles paving it and the white lap line across it (`power_tiles.gd`: one shared tile mesh drawn as a MultiMesh, so the whole track is one draw call; a step lights the tile under that foot by laying one of 8 pooled glows over it, which fades over 1.5 s; with reduced motion it stays lit for half a second, then goes dark at once without fading. The MultiMesh is never changed after it is built, because the web build's Compatibility renderer doesn't redraw instance data changed later), the Generator at its corner built from simple shapes with the cables from under the track, pump, pipe and loudspeaker lamp, which pulses faintly with each lit step while he runs (`generator.gd`), the tool rack beside it whose hoe blade and sickles change with each tools tier (`tool_rack.gd`), the weighing scale by the gate where the Overseer stands during a Harmony Audit's tell (`weighing_scale.gd`), the Worker walking out through the gate to the lap line, running laps in step with the rules' lap clock, slowing on his last lap, staggering into his breath on the lap line and going back along the track to the gate (`worker_motion.gd`, using only the model's Walk, Run, HitRecieve and Idle animations; it also flinches him on the whip, reports his speed on the track, and reports each footstep on the track with where that foot landed: two footfalls per cycle of the Walk or Run animation, at the pace it plays), the Overseer by the Generator with a whip built from simple shapes in his hand, blowing his whistle and cracking the whip (`overseer_look.gd`), tap and click picking through the camera, the time-left label, and pan and zoom: `pointer_gesture.gd` tells a tap from a drag or pinch, so only a press released in place plants or picks, and `field_camera.gd` holds the camera's pan and zoom limits; the camera's angle never changes), The App overlay (`app_overlay/`: Quota bar, Shift timer, the crop counter and the "+1" rising from a picked Plot (`rising_count.gd`; with reduced motion it fades without rising), Labour Points, the Mascot and its speech bubble, which closes after 5 s or at any tap or click without using the tap up, confetti (`confetti.gd`), the Labour Points or Debt on the bar (`balance_badge.gd`), the pay slip card (`pay_slip_card.gd`), the Hand-in card (`hand_in_card.gd`: a slider for how much of the cotton in hand to hand in, starting at all of it, that amount against the Quota, and the Hand in button), the Store button and the store it opens (`store_panel.gd`, one `store_row.gd` per item: price, tier, effect, Quota rise, why it can't be bought, and, while he has hidden savings, a pencil-on-paper button to pay from the tin), and the Study Session room in `study_room.gd`; only its Store and Settings buttons, the pay slip and Hand-in cards and the open store catch taps, and it dims, all but the room, while the Generator stands still), the hidden savings tin (`savings_tin/savings_tin.gd`: drawn in pencil on the field's left edge, under The App's layer, the only place hidden savings show), the field's sounds (`audio/field_sounds.gd`: footsteps, the power tiles' tick, the Generator's whine, made in code, the whistle and the whip crack, all on the `Field` bus from `default_bus_layout.tres`, silent until the first tap or click) and the "turn your phone sideways" cover shown in portrait.
- `src/rules/` holds the Farm rules: `Farm` with the commands `plant`, `pick`, `clear`, `run_generator`, `buy_upgrade` (`Farm.GENERATOR` or `Farm.TOOLS`), `buy_plot`, `buy_privilege` (`Farm.REST_HOUR` or `Farm.CALL_HOME`), each paid with `Farm.PAY_LABOUR_POINTS` (the default) or `Farm.PAY_HIDDEN_SAVINGS`, and `hand_in(amount)` (each returns a `CommandResult`: whether it happened, and a reason key if not), `advance(seconds)` for online play, `resume_offline(seconds)` for time away (crops grow at `offline_growth_rate` whatever the Worker was doing, a Study Session counts down, the Shift waits; negative time counts as zero and more than `offline_cap_seconds` is cut to it; it returns an `AwayReport` for the log and emits the away summary), read-only `PlotView`s (stage and time left), the `WorkerView` (in the field, running, breathing or resting, laps left before he breathes, how far through his current lap he is, how long that lap takes, and Exhaustion), `exhaustion()` and `exhaustion_floor()`, the `RestHourView` (price, seconds left, taken away), the `ShiftView` (number, Quota, picked (the crop counter), time left, the Shift whose end charges the next school fees, and whether the Hand-in is open), `hand_in_open()`, `school_fees_unpaid()` and `school_fees_unpaid_in_a_row()`, `letters_received()` (oldest first) and `letters_stopped()`, Labour Points and `debt()` (never both above zero), `hidden_savings()`, the `AuditView` from `audit()` (the tell, Shifts left on watch, times caught), and `store()` (prices and refusals worked out in `store_front.gd`): a `StoreItemView` per item, Upgrades first, then land (`Farm.LAND`: the next Plot's price and Quota rise, or fully bought), then Privileges (kind, tier owned and in all, price, next tier's effect and the owned one's, Quota rise, and the reason it can't be bought now with Labour Points, and with hidden savings (`savings_refusal`), or none). Crops grow only while the Worker runs on the Generator; after `laps_before_breath` laps he stops to breathe (a lap takes `lap_seconds` on the first land plus `lap_seconds_per_strip` for each column or row bought since, starting with his next lap; running costs `exhaustion_per_lap` over `lap_seconds` Exhaustion a second, so a longer lap costs more); after `whistle_after_seconds` the Overseer whistles and after `whip_after_seconds` more he whips and the Worker runs again (`advance()` returns these as `Farm.OVERSEER_WHISTLE` and `Farm.OVERSEER_WHIP`; the whip changes no numbers), and the laps since his last breath carry over when he leaves it. A plant or pick that happens brings him back to the field. Online play counts the Shift down whatever he is doing; at its end the Hand-in opens (`hand_in_opened`) and the Farm stands still: `advance()` moves nothing and every other command and store item is refused with `hand_in_open` until `hand_in()`, though `resume_offline()` works as usual. A pick pays nothing: it adds one to the crop counter (`ShiftView.picked`), unless it drops its cotton. `hand_in(amount)` hands in that much of the cotton in hand (all of it with no amount, `Farm.ALL_IN_HAND` or more than he holds), pays `labour_points_per_pick` for each, adds `hidden_savings_per_kept_pick` hidden savings for each pick kept back (the `handed_in` message carries `handed_in` and `kept`), then runs the rest of the Shift end: the Quota check on what was handed in, and the next Shift starts with the Quota raised by a fixed step and the counter at zero (decision 0003). A missed Quota starts a Study Session (`study_session_seconds_left()`): it takes the Worker off the Generator, plant, pick and `run_generator` are refused with `in_study_session`, crops halt, and the Shift's clock waits until it ends. Its length doubles for each miss in a row up to the tuning cap; a met Quota resets the count. Picks count from zero each Shift, so a surplus carries no credit. Exhaustion (`exhaustion.gd`, 0 to 100) rises with each plant, pick and lap; above `slow_exhaustion` field work leaves him busy for `slow_action_seconds` (refused with `worker_busy`), above `mistake_exhaustion` a pick drops its cotton when the injected roll comes up under `dropped_cotton_chance`, and the laps in a run fall towards `fewest_laps_before_breath`, set when the run starts. The Generator's laps and breaths live in `toil.gd`, and the Shift's clock, its Quota check, Study Sessions and the order of the Shift end in `shifts.gd`, both owned by Farm. Labour Points and Debt live in a Ledger (`ledger.gd`) that Farm owns, as one signed balance, so earnings pay Debt down first; Negligence docks it never below zero, so it never creates Debt. Beside it the Ledger keeps the hidden savings, never below zero: each Bill draws on Labour Points, then hidden savings, and only the rest becomes Debt (the pay slip's `<bill>_from_savings` values, logged on each `bill charged` line and never shown by The App); they never pay existing Debt or get docked, and a store purchase draws on whichever was chosen, refused with `in_debt` either way and with `not_enough_hidden_savings` (which The App never says; the tin's button does) when they fall short. Harmony Audits live in `audits.gd`, owned by Farm and run by Shifts: it remembers the last `audit_memory_shifts` Shifts (kept back, what the Plots should have yielded at `plot_yield_per_shift` each, and hidden savings spent in the store, never on Bills), and at the start of each Shift after the first rolls an audit on the Farm's roll (none at a chance of 0): `audit_base_chance` plus the kept share times `audit_chance_per_kept_share` plus savings spent times `audit_chance_per_savings_spent`, at most 1, times `on_watch_chance_factor` on watch (the chance and plan ride on `shift_started` for the log). A planned audit shows its tell for the Shift's last `audit_tell_seconds`, emits `audit_announced` when the Hand-in opens, and at the Hand-in, after the Quota check, works out the kept share with this Shift included: at or below `audit_tolerance` (0 on watch) it emits `audit_passed`; above it he is caught (`caught`, then `caught_again` for every later catch): all hidden savings are taken, the `caught_study_session_seconds` Study Session starts in place of a missed Quota's, he is on watch for `on_watch_shifts` Shifts (`on_watch_ended` after the last) and the record is wiped. At each Shift's end, after the Quota check and before the Exhaustion floor rises, the Ledger charges `electricity_per_lap` for each lap run that Shift (counted in `toil.gd`, rounded to whole Labour Points), then `rent_per_shift`, then `school_fees` at the end of every `school_fees_every_shifts`-th Shift, and emits the `pay_slip` message; school fees that leave a shortfall set "school fees unpaid" until the Debt is cleared and add one to the unpaid-in-a-row count, which covered fees reset; a shortfall becomes Debt (`fell_into_debt`), and earnings that pay off the last of it emit `debt_cleared`. A letter arrives at the start of every Shift whose number divides by `letter_every_shifts` (`letters.gd`): the one at the count of letter Shifts passed in `Farm.LETTER_IDS`, so a letter that came due while they were stopped is skipped for good, and none come once the list runs out. The letters are stopped exactly while "school fees unpaid" is set. These come out of `take_family_events()` as `FamilyEvent`s (`Farm.LETTER_ARRIVED` with the letter id, `Farm.LETTERS_STOPPED`, `Farm.LETTERS_STARTED_AGAIN`), apart from the App messages, which are the state's voice. The rest hour costs `rest_hour_price`, lasts `rest_hour_seconds` of online play (it waits while away, like the Shift) and lowers Exhaustion by `rest_hour_recovery`, never below the floor, which rises by `exhaustion_floor_rise` at each Shift's end. The call home costs `call_home_price` and lasts `call_home_seconds` (`call_home_seconds_left()`): he is off the Generator, so crops halt, field work and `run_generator` are refused with `on_a_call`, it takes no Exhaustion away, and it waits while away. Buying it sends a `Farm.CALLED_HOME` Family event whose id (one of `Farm.CALL_IDS`) is the call `Farm.LETTER_CALLS` gives for the latest letter received, or the first call before any letter, and the `call_started` App message. A rest hour and a call can't overlap. A missed Quota takes every Privilege away (`privileges_taken_away`, and `RestHourView.taken_away`) for the next Shift, and a Study Session cuts a rest or a call short. The Upgrades owned live in `store.gd`: each Generator tier in the tuning table's `generator_tiers` has a price, a `growth_multiplier` (growth per second of running against no Upgrade) that works at once, and a `quota_rise` that counts from the next Shift: a Shift's Quota is the first Quota, plus the fixed step per Shift, plus the rise of every tier bought before it began. Each tools tier in `tools_tiers` has a price, a `work_share` (the share of a slow pick's time and of the drop chance left, against no Upgrade; it never rises from tier to tier) that works at once, and a `quota_rise` counted the same way. Upgrades are refused with `not_enough_labour_points`, `in_study_session`, `in_debt` or `fully_upgraded`, and Privileges with `in_debt` too, and stay bought after a missed Quota. `Farm.new(tuning, roll)` starts the land from the tuning table (`land.gd`: the Farm's columns and rows and the cell of every Plot, which never changes; the first land's Plots are numbered row by row from the back left; `land()` gives it as a `LandView`; `buy_plot()` takes the next cell in a fixed order, a column on the right, then a row at the back, each strip from the front or the left, until the largest land; a strip's first Plot widens the land to take in the whole strip; each Plot costs more than the last and raises the Quota from the next Shift, and is refused with `fully_bought`, `in_study_session`, `in_debt` or `not_enough_labour_points`) and takes the roll as a Callable returning 0 to 1, so tests decide every dropped pick. What The App should say comes out of `take_messages()` as `AppMessage`s: a key plus values, never text. `to_save()` gives the whole Farm as plain data with a format version (`Farm.SAVE_VERSION`; raise it when the format changes; version 2 added the store, and a version 1 save restores with no Upgrades; version 3 added the tools, and a version 2 save restores with none; version 4 added the Bills, and a version 3 save restores with nothing earned and no laps run yet that Shift; version 5 added the school fees, and an older save restores with them paid up and due `school_fees_every_shifts` Shifts after its own; version 6 added the letters, and an older save restores with none received and no letter Shifts passed; version 7 added the call home, and an older save restores with none under way; version 8 added land (`plots_bought`; the cells follow from the fixed order), and an older save restores with the first land's Plots only; version 9 added longer laps (the lap in progress and its length), and an older save's run is counted in `lap_seconds` laps; version 10 added the Hand-in (`hand_in_open`), and an older save carries on with the Shift's picks as cotton in hand and no Hand-in open; version 11 added `hidden_savings`, and an older save restores with none; version 12 added Harmony Audits (`audits`: the remembered Shifts, a planned audit, on watch and times caught), and an older save restores with a clean record, none planned, never caught; a negative balance restores as Debt), and `restore()` takes it back, returning one line per bad field (checked by `save_reader.gd`) and leaving the Farm untouched if there are any. Plain GDScript objects with no scene tree, clock or file access, tested through their public interface with the fast tuning table in `tests/rules/fast_tuning.gd`.

## Debugging

Log lines go to Godot's output (the terminal running `godot`, or the browser console for the web build), one line per event: the level, the event, then `key=value` fields, for example:

```
[info] game started version="dev build"
[info] tuning loaded path="res://data/tuning.tres"
```

Plant, pick and sending the Worker to the Generator are logged at debug level, including refusals with their reason, for example `[debug] plant plot=3`, `[debug] pick refused plot=3 reason=&"not_ripe"` or `[debug] run generator`. So is each change in what the Worker is doing: `[debug] worker activity="running"`, `"breathing"` or `"in_field"`, and each thing the Overseer does: `[debug] overseer event=&"overseer_whistle"` or `&"overseer_whip"`. The first tap that lets sound play logs `[info] sound unlocked`. Each Hand-in is logged at info level as it opens and when he hands in: `[info] hand-in opened shift=1 in_hand=8 quota=10` and `[info] handed in shift=1 handed_in=6 kept=2 earned=12`. Each Quota check is logged at info level: `[info] quota checked shift=1 picked=8 quota=10 met=false`. So is each Study Session, with its length and how many in a row: `[info] study session started seconds=300.0 minutes=5 in_a_row=1` and `[info] study session ended in_a_row=1`. Each return from offline time (a hidden tab shown again, or Skip time) is logged at info level: `[info] offline resume seconds=600.0 ripened=2 study_seconds_served=0.0 exhaustion_recovered=1.7`. Each purchase is logged at info level with its item, tier and price (`[info] upgrade bought item=&"generator" tier=1 price=40`, `[info] privilege bought item=&"rest_hour" price=20`, and a Plot with its number: `[info] plot bought plot=13 price=50`), a refused one at debug (`[debug] purchase refused item=&"rest_hour" reason=&"not_enough_labour_points"`). One paid from the tin also logs `[info] hidden savings spent item=&"generator" amount=40`. Harmony Audits are logged at info level when planned, with their chance (`[info] audit planned shift=4 chance=0.24`; the plan is secret, so only the log shows it), with their result (`[info] audit passed shift=4 kept_share=0.08 tolerance=0.1`, or `[info] caught shift=4 kept_share=0.31 tolerance=0.1 income_recovered=27 minutes=120 watch_shifts=3 times_caught=1`), and when being on watch ends (`[info] on watch ended shift=7`). Its end and each dropped pick are logged at info level: `[info] rest hour ended exhaustion=22.0`, `[info] cotton dropped exhaustion=81.5`. If the clock's time couldn't be used as given, a warning comes first: `[warning] offline time adjusted problem=&"negative_offline_time" seconds_away=-60.0 seconds_counted=0.0`, or `problem=&"offline_time_capped"` past the tuning's cap. Each Bill is logged at info level with whether Labour Points and hidden savings covered it and how much the savings paid: `[info] bill charged shift=1 bill="electricity" amount=6 laps=12 covered=true from_savings=0` and `[info] bill charged shift=1 bill="rent" amount=6 covered=false from_savings=2` (and every third Shift `[info] bill charged shift=3 bill="school fees" amount=30 covered=false from_savings=0`), then `[info] debt incurred debt=4` if that put the Worker in Debt; `[info] debt cleared points=1` when earnings pay the last of it. An App message with no text logs `[warning] app message has no text key=...` (the content test should catch that first).

Saving logs where the save is and what happened to it, never the save itself: `[info] new game path="user://save.json"` or `[info] save loaded path="user://save.json" shift=3` on start, each save at debug level (`[debug] game saved reason="command"`, or `"start"`, `"shift_end"`, `"hidden"`, `"timer"`), and a failed write at error level (`[error] save failed reason=... problem=...`). On desktop the save is in Godot's user folder (on Linux `~/.local/share/godot/app_userdata/Happy Cotton/`); in the browser it is in the site's IndexedDB (the browser's developer tools show it), so clearing site data deletes it.

Crops grow (while he runs) and the Shift counts down on frame time, and Godot caps one frame's step at about 0.13 s, so below roughly 8 frames a second (a software-rendered browser, a struggling phone) crops grow slower than the wall clock. A hidden tab stops frames, so it counts as offline: the wall clock reports the hidden time when the tab is shown again.

Warnings and errors go through `push_warning`/`push_error`, so they also show in the editor's Debugger panel. Tests capture lines by setting `GameLog.sink`; set `GameLog.minimum_level = GameLog.Level.DEBUG` to see debug lines.

Known failure modes:

- **`Cannot start Happy Cotton:` on screen** — the tuning table has a missing or out-of-range value; the message names it. Fix `data/tuning.tres`.
- **`check: FAILED at: type check`** — a script has an untyped declaration or an unsafe access; the lines above say which file and line.
- **Export fails with "No export template found"** — run `scripts/setup-godot.sh`.
- **`update-site: FAILED while <step>`** — the step names what broke (fetching, building the image, exporting, swapping folders); the live site is unchanged unless it says "swapping folders". Fix it and run the script again.
- **"Your saved game couldn't be read"** — the log line `[warning] save unreadable problem=... kept_as=...` says what was wrong (not JSON, or which fields) and where the file was moved. Start over begins a new game; the damaged file stays where `kept_as` says. A save from a newer build than the one running (after a rollback) shows up this way too, as a version problem.
- **"Happy Cotton can't start in this browser"** — the browser has no WebGL 2 (or lacks another feature the message names).
