# rss-news

A personal news briefing: it reads many sources for one owner and writes a short Briefing on request, so the owner only opens an Item when something catches their interest.

## Language

### What gets read

**Source**:
One place Items come from: an RSS or Atom feed, a subreddit, one newsletter sender, or a ticker's news.
_Avoid_: Feed, channel, subscription

**Folder**:
A group of Sources made by the owner, such as AI, World, Macro, Stocks, Malaysia or Tech. Each Source is in exactly one Folder.
_Avoid_: Topic, category, section, tag

**Item**:
One piece of news taken from a Source, such as an article, a Reddit post or a newsletter issue. It belongs to its Source's Folder.
_Avoid_: Article, post, entry, story

**Importance**:
A score from 1 to 5 for how much an Item matters to the owner, given when the Item arrives.
_Avoid_: Priority, rank, relevance

**Inbox**:
The list of Items, unread ones latest first unless the owner asks for read ones, all of them, or most important first; it can be filtered by Folder, Importance and search words.
_Avoid_: Feed, timeline, stream

**Reader view**:
An Item's full article, fetched from the original page and cleaned of ads and clutter when the owner opens the Item. Nothing of it is kept.
_Avoid_: Full text, article cache

### What gets written

**Chat**:
A conversation with the AI about the owner's Items. Its answers list the matching Items, each with a one-line summary and a Source button that opens that Item. Selected text can be brought into the Chat.
_Avoid_: Ask AI, assistant, bot

**Briefing**:
A short written overview the owner asks for, covering all Sources or one Folder over the last 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days, or a set of Items picked by hand. Each point links to the Items it came from.
_Avoid_: Digest, summary, recap, newsletter

**Simple summary**:
One whole Item explained in plain words, as if to someone new to the subject: a one-sentence gist and three to six short points. Written when the owner presses Summarise in the Item view, and not kept. Not the Item's one-line summary, which scoring writes for every Item.
_Avoid_: ELI5, TL;DR, explainer

**Dictionary**:
A short definition of a selected word or phrase, fitted to the text around it, including jargon, stock tickers and Malay words.
_Avoid_: Glossary, lookup, explain

**Selection popup**:
The buttons that appear under text the owner selects in a Briefing, an Item or a Chat answer: Ask AI (opens the Chat with the selection and where it came from), Note and Dictionary.
_Avoid_: Context menu, toolbar

**Note**:
Text the owner selected and saved, with an optional comment and a link back to where it came from.
_Avoid_: Highlight, bookmark, clip

**Alert**:
A push notification about one Item, sent because the Item mentions a Watchlist stock or has Importance 5.
_Avoid_: Notification, ping, breaking news

**Test Alert**:
An Alert the owner sends from Settings to check that Alerts reach the phone. It opens the newest Item, and isn't about a stock or Importance.

**Push subscription**:
Where one browser receives Alerts, given by the browser when the owner turns Alerts on for that phone. One per phone; the push service can say it is gone, and then it is deleted.
_Avoid_: Device, registration, token

### The owner's stocks

**Watchlist**:
The stocks the owner follows, whether they own them or not.
_Avoid_: Wishlist, Holdings, portfolio
