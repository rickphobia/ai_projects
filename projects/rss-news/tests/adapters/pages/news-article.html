Malaysia's central bank holds rates as ringgit firms

By A. Reporter, Kuala Lumpur

Bank Negara Malaysia kept its overnight policy rate at 2.75 per cent on Thursday, saying inflation remained moderate and the economy was growing at a steady pace through the third quarter.

The ringgit strengthened slightly against the US dollar after the decision, extending gains made earlier in the month as foreign investors returned to Malaysian government bonds.

Economists had widely expected the hold. Several said the central bank was likely to keep rates unchanged into next year unless global demand for electronics exports weakened sharply.

The bank said it would continue to watch fuel subsidy changes, which could push up prices in the months ahead, and would act if the outlook for inflation shifted.
